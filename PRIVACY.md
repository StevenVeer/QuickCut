# QuickCut Privacy Policy

*Last updated: November 4, 2026*

QuickCut is published by Ringside. This policy explains what QuickCut does with your information.

## Short version

QuickCut does not collect, store or send any personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no
analytics and no tracking, and it never connects to the internet on its own.

## What stays on your PC

To work, QuickCut uses some information on your own computer. None of it leaves your PC.

- **Your settings** (your buttons, shortcut and preferences) are saved in a file at
  `%APPDATA%\QuickCut\config.ini`. You can delete it at any time.
- **Installed apps and their icons** are read so you can pick an app for a button.
- **Open windows** are read when you use a button that switches between windows.
- **What's playing** (title, artist and cover art from apps like Spotify or your browser) is
  read from Windows to show under the ring and to control playback.
- **System volume** is read and changed when you use the volume controls.

## When something is sent

QuickCut only opens your web browser when you ask it to, for example a website button you set up
yourself, or **Report a bug** / **Request a feature** in Settings.

The report links open a page on GitHub with the QuickCut version and your Windows version filled
in, so a problem is easier to fix. Nothing is sent until you choose to submit that page yourself.
What you submit there is handled by GitHub under
[GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Microsoft Store

If you install QuickCut from the Microsoft Store, Microsoft may collect information about the
download and install as described in the
[Microsoft Privacy Statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement). Ringside receives
only the anonymous totals that Microsoft shows every publisher, such as download counts.

## Children

QuickCut collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
